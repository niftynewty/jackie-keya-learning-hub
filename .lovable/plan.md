# Make the interface feel deliberately human-made

## Goal
Remove generic AI-design signals across the public site, student area, booking flow, and Admin Studio while preserving the warm coffee-brown editorial mood board and the certificate artwork it inspired.

## Site-wide visual cleanup
- Remove gradients, glass blur, decorative shadows, glow effects, radial orbs, dot grids, neon/purple styling, and decorative animation.
- Replace pure white surfaces with the existing soft cream linen and warm paper tones.
- Tighten corners to crisp editorial edges. Keep circles only where their shape communicates purpose, such as avatars and the certificate seal.
- Keep the current Manrope and DM Serif Display typography because neither is on the excluded font list and both support the mood board.
- Preserve the restrained coffee, oat, terracotta, cream, and deep-roast palette rather than introducing rainbow or generic pastel colors.

## Icons and motion
- Remove Lucide icons from visible page, dashboard, booking, profile, and admin interfaces.
- Replace necessary controls with clear text labels or restrained typographic symbols only where the meaning remains obvious.
- Remove animated arrows and widespread hover movement. Keep only subtle state feedback needed to show that links and controls are interactive.
- Remove checkmark-led marketing bullets and replace them with plain editorial lists or numbered details.

## Layout and content patterns
- Break up obvious three-card feature rows and bento-style groupings into two-column, four-column, stacked, table, or editorial list layouts appropriate to the content.
- Remove the anonymous “Learning Hub student” testimonial because it cannot be verified. Keep Jackie’s attributed editorial quotation.
- Remove empty Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links or pages if present.
- Remove “it’s not X, it’s Y” phrasing, emoji, terminal-style panels, three-tier pricing layouts, and fake product-demo treatments wherever found.
- Retain genuine working previews such as certificate previews, course progress, booking calendars, and the learning interface because they demonstrate the real product.

## Mood-board exceptions
- Keep the certificate templates’ intentional curved ribbons, arches, medallions, serif typography, and warm paper field because they directly follow the approved certificate inspiration.
- Keep functional circular profile photos and status indicators where changing the shape would reduce clarity.
- Preserve the dark coffee Admin Studio navigation because it belongs to the approved warm editorial system, not a purple-and-black theme.

## Validation
- Check every public and signed-in screen at desktop and mobile widths for forbidden patterns, layout collisions, and legibility.
- Confirm navigation, course enrollment, booking, profiles, certificate issuing/downloads, and Admin Studio controls still work after icon and styling changes.
- Run the project type check and inspect the live browser console before completion.
